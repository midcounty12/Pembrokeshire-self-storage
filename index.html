Privacy & data protection — DRAFT

Pembrokeshire Self Storage Ltd will use customer information to manage enquiries, bookings, contracts, payments, security and customer service. Information may include contact details, booking/container records, payment status, signed agreements, correspondence, ID-verification status and CCTV images.

Information may be shared where necessary with service providers supporting hosting, identity verification, electronic agreements, transactional email and GoCardless payments, and with professional advisers or public authorities where legally required.

The final live notice must confirm the company's full legal/contact details, actual providers, retention periods, international transfers where relevant, lawful bases and customers' data-protection rights. Raw identity documents should not be retained by the website where a specialist verification provider can process them instead.

Cookie notice — DRAFT

The live website may use necessary cookies for login, sessions and security. Analytics or advertising cookies must not be placed until the appropriate consent mechanism is implemented. The final policy should identify each cookie/provider, its purpose and duration.
